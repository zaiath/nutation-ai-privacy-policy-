Delete your Nutrition AI account

To delete your account and data:
1. Open Nutrition AI and go to Settings.
2. Tap "Delete account" and confirm.
3. Or email info@codearter.com from the email address on your account
   with the subject "Delete my account". We respond within 30 days.

Deleted: your account, profile (age, height, weight, goals), meal logs,
workout logs, and sign-in details.

Kept: limited records we must keep by law, or to prevent fraud or resolve
disputes. These are deleted when no longer needed, and backups are removed
on their normal cycle (up to 90 days).
