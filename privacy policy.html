# Nutrition AI Privacy Policy **Effective date:** 11/11/2026 **App:** Nutrition AI **Developer:** Rizqorah LLC (d/b/a CodeArter) **Contact:** info@codearter.com This policy is a release draft and should be reviewed before publication. It is written specifically for Nutrition AI and must be hosted at a public URL that is entered in Google Play Console and App Store Connect. ## Information we collect Depending on the features you use, Nutrition AI may collect: - account information such as email address and authentication identifiers; - health and fitness profile information that you choose to enter, such as age, height, weight, activity level, health goals, fasting glucose, and HbA1c; - meal descriptions, nutrition results, meal history, workout entries, and analytics; - device, app, crash, and diagnostic information needed to operate and improve the app; - advertising-related information handled by Google Mobile Ads when ads are enabled and permitted by your consent choices. Nutrition AI does not access contacts, call logs, SMS, microphone, or location. Camera access is not used unless a later version explicitly adds a camera feature and updates this policy and the store disclosures. ## How we use information We use information to: - authenticate your account; - calculate nutrition estimates, calorie targets, glycemic estimates, workout estimates, and trends; - save and display your meal, workout, and profile history across supported devices; - operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service; - display advertisements where the app has enabled advertising and applicable consent has been obtained. Nutrition AI provides general wellness estimates. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Do not use the app to make medication or emergency health decisions. ## Service providers Depending on the deployed configuration, information may be processed by: - Google Firebase Authentication and Firebase services for account and application data; - the deployed Nutrition AI backend and its configured database provider for synchronization; - Google Mobile Ads for advertising and consent management; - food or nutrition data/AI providers used by the backend to resolve food descriptions. The current production provider list must be kept synchronized with the actual deployment before store submission. ## Storage and security We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including HTTPS for network traffic and access controls for application services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Do not enter information you are not comfortable storing with the service. ## Sharing and selling We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with the service providers listed above only as needed to operate the service, comply with law, prevent abuse, or protect users and the service. ## Retention and deletion We retain account and health/fitness records while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing info@codearter.com. We may retain limited records when required by law, for fraud prevention, or for resolving disputes. ## Children Nutrition AI is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. ## Changes We may update this policy as the app or its providers change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published. ## Contact For privacy questions or deletion requests, email **info@codearter.com**.
